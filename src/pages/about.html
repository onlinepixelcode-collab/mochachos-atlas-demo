How it started

One of the first places in SA to serve Mexican

Mochachos opened its first store in Pretoria in 1994. Back then it was called Mochachos Steak Villages, and it was one of the first outlets in South Africa to put Mexican dishes on the menu.

The idea grew into what you know today: Mexican-flavoured, flame-grilled chicken alongside a proper Mexican menu of burritos, fajitas, chimichangas and nachos. People wanted something exotic that still felt good to eat, so the chicken is cooked with dry herbs and spices.

Today you'll find Mochachos across South Africa and in Botswana, Zambia, Mauritius and Australia.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Is everything halaal?

Yes. Mochachos Atlas Road is 100% halaal.

How spicy is the chicken?

You choose. Our Zee Flavours go from Lemon & Herb (no heat) through Mild, Hot and Extra Hot to Dynamite, the hottest on the board.

Can I order ahead for pick-up?

Yes. Build your order in the tray on this site, then call us on 010 448 0666 and read it out. We'll have it ready.

Do you deliver?

We're on Uber Eats and Mr D.

Are you open on Fridays?

Yes, from 10:00 to 21:00, but we close from 12:15 to 13:30 for prayers.

Can you cater for a big group?

Yes. Our family meals feed 4 to 8 people. See Groups & parties or call ahead and we'll set up the long table.

Is there parking?

Yes, on the Sasol forecourt right in front of the store. Please keep the side gate clear.
